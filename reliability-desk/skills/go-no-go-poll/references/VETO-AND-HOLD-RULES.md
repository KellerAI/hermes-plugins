# Veto and Hold Rules

How a NO-GO stops a release, who may clear it, and how a recycle to a later poll is recorded.

## The veto

Any single NO-GO holds the release.
The hold takes effect the moment the call is made, before the remaining disciplines are polled, and the poll continues so the full picture is recorded.
A veto is not weighed against the GO calls, is not overturned by headcount, and does not expire because the release is late.

The veto is attributable by construction: it belongs to the named owner of one discipline, and the record carries their name.
A poll whose disciplines have no named owners has no veto at all, only the appearance of one.

## What a hold produces

Every hold is recorded with four fields.

| Field | Content |
| ----- | ------- |
| Discipline | The console that held |
| Owner | The person who made the call |
| Reason | The specific concern, stated as a condition of the artifact rather than a feeling |
| Clearing condition | The evidence that would convert the call to GO, and who accepts it |

A hold with no clearing condition is unfinished.
The owner who held is responsible for stating what would satisfy them.

## Who may clear a hold

Only the owner who raised the NO-GO may clear it, on presentation of the evidence named in the clearing condition.
A different person may clear it only under a recorded override that names the person accepting the risk, the reason, and the residual exposure.
An override is a decision to launch with a known open concern, and it is recorded as such, never as a GO.

Nobody clears a hold by re-explaining the concern to the owner.
The clearing condition is evidence, not persuasion.

## Recycle

When a hold is cleared, the release recycles to a new poll rather than resuming the old one.
Every discipline is polled again from the top, because the fix that cleared one console may have moved another console's ground.

The recycle is recorded as a distinct poll with its own timestamp, its own roster, and its own set of calls.
The prior poll and its hold stay in the record; they are not edited or replaced.
A release that recycles repeatedly on the same discipline is a signal that the underlying work is not ready, not that the poll is obstructive.

## Abort

If a clearing condition cannot be met within the release window, the correct outcome is to stand down and re-plan, not to re-poll until the answer changes.
Record the stand-down with the discipline that held and what remains open, so the next attempt starts from evidence rather than from fatigue.
